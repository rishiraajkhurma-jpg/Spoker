# A2 outreach templates

Fill `{{ }}` from the event profile, the A0 rate card and the contact row. The code refuses any email that still contains a `{{placeholder}}`, an em or en dash, no opt-out sentence, or no postal address in the signature. Numbers must come from the event profile with their source; never invent a result.

`{{signature}}` = the full block from `sponsordesk-email` (with postal address).

Rule of the first email: an offer they could forward to their boss. The Crustdata signal chooses the idea; the email leads with the idea, price and metric.
