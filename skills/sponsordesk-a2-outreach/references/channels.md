# Outreach channels (beyond mass email)

Mass mailing in the "same email to 500 people" sense is not used: it burns the manager's domain and the event's name. "At scale" here means many individually personalized emails, capped per day.

| Channel | Best for | How the agent helps | Risk / rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Warm intro via team or alumni | P1, big brands | Finds the alum (Crustdata education filter), drafts a forwardable intro blurb the alum can paste | Ask the alum first, never cc them unasked |
| Referral ask to a senior person | When the right owner is unclear | T0-D template | One message, no pitch |
| Personalized cold email | Default at scale | Full A2 flow | Caps, opt-out, verified emails only |
| Past-sponsor re-entry | Lapsed sponsors | Reference the past edition, results, and what is new | Never pitch them as if new |
| Peer-fest sponsor scan | Finding warm-budget brands | Pull sponsor lists from other fests' sites | Public pages only |
| Speaker-to-sponsor | Companies whose leaders speak at E-Summit | Speaker invite first, sponsorship conversation later via their team | Never make a speaking slot conditional on money unless policy allows |
| Hiring signal play | Companies with many open grad roles | Email talent acquisition with the internship fair angle | |
| Event-in-common | People attending the same conference/meetup | Manager meets them in person; agent preps a one-page brief | |
| Product-led (in-kind first) | D2C, dev tools | Offer a hackathon track or sampling first, upsell to cash | In-kind cap 50% without approval |
| Student ambassador intro | Brands with campus ambassador programs | Their ambassador on our campus forwards to the brand manager | |
| Inbound page | Always on | A "Partner with E-Summit" page by the manager | Low yield, never the plan |
| Phone call (business line) | P1 after an email is opened by a reply or warm path | Call script with 3 questions, logged as a touch | Only listed business numbers; respect DND/TRAI rules |
| WhatsApp | Only after the person shares their number and agrees | Short confirmations, never cold | Never cold WhatsApp |

Priority for a given company: warm intro > referral ask > cold email > others.
